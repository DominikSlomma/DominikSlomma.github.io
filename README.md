# Dominik Slomma

Hi, I am Dominik Slomma, a PhD student at the Robotics Institute, University of Technology Sydney.

My research is focussed on robotics, computer vision, visual SLAM, and perception-driven autonomy. I am especially interested in real-time systems that help robots understand complex environments reliably, with current work centred on surgical robotics for minimally invasive procedures.

Before starting my PhD in Sydney, I worked with the [Westphalian University of Applied Sciences](https://www.en.w-hs.de/about-us/the-university/) and the [German Rescue Robotics Center](https://rettungsrobotik.de/) on rescue robotics and drone-based situational awareness systems. This work sat in a collaborative environment with emergency-service users, industry partners, universities, and research institutes. It included deploying research prototypes into a robotics command vehicle, testing them with emergency services in exercises, and supporting real deployments with aerial imagery, mapping, and 3D scene information.

I also have an engineering background in embedded systems and real-time test automation. Before moving deeper into robotics research, I worked as a project engineer at Gesellschaft für Verbrennungsmotoren und Hybridantriebe in Dortmund, focussing on control-unit development, hardware and software testing, automotive exhaust aftertreatment systems, communication analysis, and hardware-in-the-loop validation.

This GitHub Pages repository hosts my personal academic website, including my research projects, publications, and recent updates.

## Research Interests

- Visual SLAM and 3D reconstruction
- Surgical robotics and minimally invasive procedures
- Rescue robotics and UAV-based perception
- Real-time computer vision
- GPU/CPU-efficient robotics systems
- Deployment-ready robotic systems
